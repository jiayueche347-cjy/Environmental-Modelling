# Environmental Modelling

Selected environmental modelling projects completed as part of the MSc in Environmental Engineering at the Technical University of Denmark (DTU).

This repository showcases applications of numerical modelling to water quality, contaminant transport, groundwater systems, and wastewater treatment.

## Projects

### 1. River Water Quality & CSO Modelling
Analysis and modelling of pollutant emissions from combined sewer overflows (CSOs) to the Mølle Å river system, including model testing, sensitivity analysis, and simulation of water-quality impacts.

### 2. ODE-Based Contaminant Fate Modelling
Application of ordinary differential equations (ODEs) to simulate contaminant adsorption, sequestration, biodegradation, and microbial growth using analytical and numerical approaches.

### 3. Riverbank Filtration & Contaminant Transport
Modelling of groundwater flow and contaminant transport in a riverbank filtration system, including hydraulic travel time, reactive transport, degradation, and breakthrough behaviour.

### 4. Wastewater NH₄⁺ Modelling
Data-driven modelling of ammonium dynamics in wastewater systems, including sensitivity analysis, parameter estimation, model calibration, and performance evaluation.

## Methods & Tools

- Python
- Numerical modelling
- Ordinary differential equations (ODEs)
- Groundwater and contaminant transport modelling
- Sensitivity analysis
- Monte Carlo simulation
- Parameter estimation and model calibration
- Data analysis and visualisation
