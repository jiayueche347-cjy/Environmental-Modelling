# Environmental Modelling Portfolio

A collection of environmental modelling projects completed as part of the MSc Environmental Engineering programme at the Technical University of Denmark (DTU).

These projects cover water-quality modelling, contaminant fate, groundwater reactive transport, wastewater modelling, sensitivity analysis, and model calibration.

The portfolio demonstrates experience with environmental model development, simulation, sensitivity analysis, parameter estimation, calibration and validation, and interpretation of model results.

---

## Projects

### 01 — River Water Quality & CSO Modelling

Modelling the impact of combined sewer overflows (CSOs) on micropollutant concentrations in the Mølle Å river system.

**Highlights**
- River water-quality and mass-balance modelling
- Spatial simulation of pollutant concentrations
- Environmental Quality Standard (EQS) exceedance analysis
- Model diagnosis and sensitivity analysis

➡️ [View project](./01-river-water-quality/)

---

### 02 — ODE-Based Contaminant Fate Modelling

Using ordinary differential equations to simulate contaminant adsorption, sequestration, and microbial biodegradation.

**Highlights**
- ODE-based environmental modelling
- Reversible and irreversible contaminant processes
- Analytical and numerical solutions
- Python and Excel implementation
- Microbial growth and biodegradation modelling

➡️ [View project](./02-contaminant-fate-ode/)

---

### 03 — Riverbank Filtration & Reactive Contaminant Transport

Modelling groundwater flow and reactive contaminant transport in a riverbank filtration system.

**Highlights**
- Two-dimensional groundwater and contaminant transport modelling
- Advection, dispersion, retardation, and degradation
- Breakthrough-curve analysis for PMT substances
- Pumping-well scenario analysis
- Time-dependent contaminant input
- Model uncertainty and assumption assessment

➡️ [View project](./03-riverbank-filtration/)

---

### 04 — Wastewater Ammonium Model Calibration

Calibration and validation of an NH₄⁺ model for wastewater and sewer-system applications.

**Highlights**
- Local and global sensitivity analysis
- Morris global sensitivity analysis
- Sensitivity-guided parameter selection
- Global optimization using dual annealing
- Model calibration and independent validation
- Parameter-bound and model-performance assessment

➡️ [View project](./04-wastewater-ammonium-calibration/)
